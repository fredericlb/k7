# Exercises

**Exercises** (`/rehearsal/exercices`) holds three practice tools, each opened from a card.

## Singing exercises

`/rehearsal/exercices/singing` is the band's common book of warm-ups and drills. Everyone signed in
sees all of them and can improve any of them; only the person who wrote an exercise can delete it.

- **Add an exercise** opens a form: a **Title** everyone will recognise (for example "Lip trills,
  five notes up"), a description of how it goes (range, tempo, what to watch for), and an optional
  **Demo**, which must be a YouTube link (`youtube.com`, `m.youtube.com`, `music.youtube.com` or
  `youtu.be`).
- Click an exercise to edit it in the same form.
- **Create a workout** draws three exercises at random, to work in the order given. **Roll again**
  draws three others. When the book holds fewer than three, the workout is the whole book.

## Random chords

`/rehearsal/exercices/random-chords` calls out a random chord (**Chord #N**) for you to play on
your instrument: any of the twelve roots with one of fourteen qualities (major, minor, dominant
7th, major 7th, minor 7th, sixth, minor sixth, diminished, augmented, suspended 2nd, suspended
4th, ninth, added 9th, half-diminished).

Play it, then **Reveal fingering** to check yourself against the guitar diagram, and **Next chord**.

## Guitar tuner

`/rehearsal/exercices/tuner` tunes a guitar in standard tuning (E A D G B E).

1. Click **Start tuning** and allow the microphone.
2. Play a single open string.
3. The tuner shows the note it hears, how many cents off it is, and the nearest string with its
   frequency. Tune until the note turns green ("in tune") and the needle is centred.
