# Where these models come from

## guitarml proteus/

GuitarML's ToneLibrary, `Proteus/`: https://github.com/GuitarML/ToneLibrary (commit d32a73f5), distributed
under the repository's GPL-3.0 licence. Proteus models, LSTM 40, trained at 44.1 kHz. The ones whose name
ends in `…Knob` (GainKnob, DriveKnob, BoostKnob, VolKnob, DistKnob) were trained with that knob: it is
`Knob 1` here. The others are one setting, captured.

## aida-x deer ink studios/

AIDA DSP's `aidadsp-lv2`, `models/deer ink studios/`: https://github.com/AidaDSP/aidadsp-lv2 (commit f86e21dc).
Egnater Tweaker 40 captures by deer ink studios, AIDA-X models, LSTM 12, 48 kHz, no knobs. Licence, as each
file's own metadata states: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 — non-commercial use, credit the author, no modified versions.

## fender super reverb/, roland jc 120b/, two rocks/

NAM captures (`.nam`) that were already in this library; where they were downloaded from was not recorded.
Each file names the amp, its settings and the microphones. Add the source and licence here once known.
