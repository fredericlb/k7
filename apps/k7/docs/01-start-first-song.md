# Your first song

Five minutes from signing in to hearing something you made. Nothing here needs the command bar or a
text file; every step is a click or a drag.

## 1. Open a demo

The projects page lists your **workspaces** — folders that each hold several projects. The
**Examples** workspace holds demo songs, marked **demo — resets**. Click one to open it.

A demo is put back as it shipped each time k7 restarts. To keep what you change, use **Copy to my
workspace** in the yellow line under the header: it copies the demo into one of your own workspaces
and opens the copy.

## 2. Press play

The **Play** button sits in the middle of the header, with **Stop**, the clock, **Loop** and
**Record** beside it. **Space** does the same as Play.

To make sound, k7 needs an **audio engine**. The engine is chosen in one place: the foot of the
sidebar (the **☰** at the left of a project's header opens it), which says where the sound comes out,
with a **Change** button and the engine's figures right below:

- **Sound on <machine>** — an engine on that computer is playing, through *its* speakers or audio
  interface. If that is not the computer in front of you, you will not hear it here.
- **Sound in this browser** — the server's engine is playing, streamed to this page.
- **No sound — choose an engine** — nothing is playing yet. Start the k7 tray on this
  computer and open k7 from its menu, or press **Change** and pick an engine from the list.

The project opens and stays editable either way; the bar along the bottom says what it is still
waiting for.

## 3. Make your own

Back on the projects page, **New project** on a workspace offers five kinds:

| Kind | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Song | a timeline with one synth track — record, write and arrange |
| DJ set | two turntables and a crossfader, for mixing tracks live |
| Video | a timeline for video clips and their sound, and a window showing the picture |
| Live input | a mic or guitar played through effects, with no timeline |
| Live video | the same, plus your webcam on screen |

Any of them can grow tracks, effects or video later. For a **Live** project, use headphones: what
comes in goes straight to the speakers.

## 4. Add sound and picture

**Drag files from your computer onto a lane** of the timeline. Audio files dropped together go one
per lane, top to bottom; videos dropped together play one after another on the lane you dropped them
on. A vertical phone video turns a new video project vertical to match.

- **Move** a clip by dragging it; hold **Shift** to move it off the grid.
- **Delete** a clip: click it, then press **Delete** or **Backspace**.
- **Cut** a clip in two: the blade tool (**b**), then click where to cut. Nothing is re-recorded or
  copied — the two halves play the same file.
- **Zoom** with **Ctrl/Cmd + scroll** or a trackpad pinch; plain scrolling moves up and down the
  tracks, **Shift + scroll** sideways.
- **Undo** with **Ctrl/Cmd + Z**; the message says what was undone.

The tempo and time signature are at the bottom right — click either to change it.

## 5. Record

Press **Record**, then **Play**. Record arms the track under the caret, or the only track there is,
so it can take what comes in; a track needs an input to record. Press **Play** again to stop.

## 6. Keep it, share it

k7 **saves by itself** — the word *saved* next to a file means it is on disk. To take the result
somewhere else, open **Share / export**: an MP3 or WAV of the song, or, in a video project, an MP4
(or WebM where the browser cannot make MP4) at 720p or 1080p.
